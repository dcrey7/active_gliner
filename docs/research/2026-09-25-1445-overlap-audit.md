# Overlap audit - evaluation datasets vs model training data (O4)

Question: which of our 6 evaluation datasets can be inside the training data of the student, its encoder, or the teachers?

Scope: CleanCoNLL, BC5CDR, MIT Movie (Galileo fix), CrossRE, Hallmarks of Cancer, MASSIVE 1.1 (en-US, fr-FR).
Models: `fastino/gliner2.5-multi-v1` (student), GLiNER2 (ladder), mDeBERTa-v3 and DeBERTa-v3 (encoders), Gemma 4 E4B and 12B, Qwen 3.8 27B, gpt-oss-120b (teachers), original GLiNER (context only).

Method: read the papers, model cards and blogs on 25 Sep 2026. Web only. No sign-in. No probe was run. Nothing in this note is measured.

## 1. Classes

| Code | Meaning |
|---|---|
| T | Known in training. A source says so. |
| E | Known used for evaluation only. A source reports zero-shot results on it. |
| P | Possible. A named path exists (for example PubMed or Wikipedia text in the corpus), but no source confirms it. |
| N | Not possible by date. The dataset text did not exist when the corpus was collected. |
| U | Unknown. The model owner does not disclose the data, and no path is known. |

"Text" overlap means the raw sentences. "Label" overlap means the gold annotations. Text overlap is much more likely than label overlap for every pair.

## 3. Findings per model

### 3.1 Student: `fastino/gliner2.5-multi-v1`

+ Card - built on mDeBERTa-v3-base, 287M, Apache-2.0. The card names no training data and no benchmark. Source: https://huggingface.co/fastino/gliner2.5-multi-v1
+ Hub metadata - no `base_model` tag and no `dataset` tag, so the card does not say whether GLiNER2.5 starts from GLiNER2 weights. Unknown. Source: Hub search for "fastino", 25 Sep 2026 (https://huggingface.co/fastino).
+ Training data - the GLiNER2.5 blog says: "For GLiNER2.5 we generated our training data with the Fastino Data Agent instead of assembling existing datasets." The agent "picks the document layouts, writes the schemas, varies the surface forms". The blog names no teacher LLM, no source corpus and no size. It has no decontamination statement. Source: https://fastino.ai/blog/gliner2-5-span-free-information-extraction
+ Evaluation - the blog reports 16 zero-shot benchmarks: ag_news, clinc_oos, imdb, multilingual_sentiment, rotten_tomatoes, xnli, crossner_ai, crossner_literature, crossner_music, crossner_politics, crossner_science, few_nerd, german_ler, hipe2020, mobie, ronec. Source: same blog, appendix table.
+ Our sets in that list - only CrossNER (5 domains). CoNLL, BC5CDR, MIT Movie, Hallmarks, MASSIVE and SLURP do not appear, in training or in evaluation.
+ What "zero-shot on CrossNER" tells us - Fastino claims CrossNER is held out. That is weak evidence that the CrossNER sentences are not in the training mix. It is not proof: "zero-shot" means no CrossNER training labels, and synthetic data from an LLM can still reproduce Wikipedia text.
+ Link to CrossRE - CrossRE annotates relations "on top of CrossNER" and keeps "the train/dev/test data split by Liu et al.". So CrossRE sentences are CrossNER sentences, and CrossRE test is (a subset of) CrossNER test. Source: CrossRE paper, section 3, https://aclanthology.org/2022.findings-emnlp.263.pdf
+ Consequence - Fastino has seen its zero-shot score on the CrossNER test sentences that are in our CrossRE test. If Fastino chose checkpoints or mixtures on those scores, that is test-set selection on our sentences (entities, not relations). Unknown whether they did.

### 3.2 GLiNER2 (ladder model `gliner2-large-v1`, and the GLiNER2 paper)

+ Training data - "254,334 examples, combining real-world documents and synthetic data". "The real-world set consists of 135,698 documents from news articles, Wikipedia, legal texts, PubMed abstracts, and ArXiv papers." "118,636 synthetic examples with GPT-4o". "All documents were automatically annotated with GPT-4o." Source: GLiNER2 paper, section 3.1, https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.18546
+ Not named - the news corpora, the Wikipedia dump, the PubMed subset. No decontamination or benchmark exclusion step. No mention of Pile-NER or NuNER. Source: same paper.
+ Labels - all training labels come from GPT-4o, not from gold sets. So gold-label overlap is unlikely. Text overlap is possible for PubMed (BC5CDR, Hallmarks), Wikipedia (CrossRE 5 domains) and news (CoNLL, if Reuters 1996 text is in the news set; unknown).
+ Evaluation - zero-shot on CrossNER (AI, Literature, Music, Politics, Science) and on SST-2, IMDB, SNIPS, Banking77, Amazon-Intent, AG News, 20 Newsgroups. Source: same paper, section 4.
+ SNIPS note - SNIPS is a voice-assistant intent set like SLURP/MASSIVE, but it is a different dataset. It gives no evidence about MASSIVE.

### 3.3 Encoders

+ DeBERTa-v3 (English; base in gliner2.5-base and gliner2 ladder models) - "160GB data, which is the same as DeBERTaV2 and RoBERTa": "Wikipedia, Bookcorpus, CCNews, Stories and OpenWebText". Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2111.09543
  + Wikipedia in the corpus - the CrossNER / CrossRE non-news sentences come from Wikipedia (CrossRE paper, section 3). So text overlap is likely at the article level. Exact sentence match is unknown, because Wikipedia text changes over time.
  + CC-News covers September 2016 to February 2019 (RoBERTa paper, https://arxiv.org/abs/1907.11692). SLURP (2020) and MASSIVE (2022) came later. So N for MASSIVE.
  + CoNLL-2003 - DeBERTaV3 reports a CoNLL-2003 NER score (93.9 dev F1, large). That is a fine-tuned copy. The released checkpoint is pretrain only. Source: same DeBERTaV3 paper.
+ mDeBERTa-v3 (student encoder) - "trained with 2.5TB CC100 data which is the same as XLM-R". Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2111.09543
  + CC-100 uses "January-December 2018 Commoncrawl snapshots". Source: https://data.statmt.org/cc-100/
  + So MASSIVE and SLURP text cannot be in it (N). Wikipedia pages and PubMed pages can be in Common Crawl, so CrossRE, BC5CDR and Hallmarks text overlap is possible but unmeasured.
+ Encoder overlap matters less - a masked encoder sees raw text without our labels. It can make test text "familiar", but it cannot memorise our annotations.

### 3.4 Teachers

+ Gemma 4 (E4B and 12B) - card: "web documents, code, images, audio, with a cutoff date of January 2025", "Web documents across 140+ languages". No token count. Source: https://huggingface.co/google/gemma-4-E4B-it
  + Technical report: "We filter data to decontaminate benchmarks". It does not list which benchmarks. Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.02770
  + Report lists sizes 2.3B (E2B), 4.5B (E4B), 12B, 31B dense and 26B-A3.8B MoE. Source: same report.
+ Qwen 3.8 27B - the card gives no data, no token count, no cutoff and no decontamination statement. It says the model is "built on the architectural foundation of Qwen3.5". Source: https://huggingface.co/Qwen/Qwen3.8-27B
  + Family context only (not this model): Qwen3 used "36 trillion tokens" in "119 languages and dialects", plus PDF OCR text and synthetic data. Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.09388
  + Qwen2 removed training text by n-gram and LCS match against its own benchmarks (MMLU, GPQA, HumanEval, GSM8k, MATH, IFEval and others). Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.10671. None of our sets is in that list.
+ gpt-oss-120b - "a text-only dataset with trillions of tokens, with a focus on STEM, coding, and general knowledge". Knowledge cutoff June 2024. No decontamination statement. Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.10925
+ Dates - all our datasets were public before every teacher cutoff: CoNLL-03 (2003), MIT Movie (2013, files at http://sls.csail.mit.edu/downloads/movie/), BC5CDR (2016), Hallmarks (2016), SLURP (2020, text on GitHub under CC BY 4.0, https://github.com/pswietojanski/slurp), MASSIVE (April 2022, https://arxiv.org/abs/2204.08582), CrossRE (2022), CleanCoNLL labels (2023, https://arxiv.org/abs/2310.16225).
+ Decontamination, where stated, targets the owners' own benchmarks (math, code, knowledge). No teacher owner says it decontaminated NER, relation, biomedical classification or slot-filling sets. So stated decontamination gives us no protection.

### 3.5 Known contamination evidence for our sets

+ CoNLL-2003, strong - the LM Contamination Index marks ChatGPT as "contaminated" on CoNLL03 train, dev and test. Source: https://github.com/hitz-zentroa/lm-contamination (data.json, row "ChatGPT / CoNLL03").
  + The blog shows ChatGPT generating the first train example with correct labels ("EU is an organization, German and British are miscellaneous, Peter Blackburn is a person and BRUSSELS is a location"). It also says "the model is able to generate the validation and even the test splits, including annotation errors such as China labeled as a person". Source: https://hitz-zentroa.github.io/lm-contamination/blog/
  + Position paper: Sainz et al. 2023, "NLP Evaluation in trouble". Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2310.18018
  + That evidence is for ChatGPT, not for our teachers. For Gemma 4, Qwen 3.8 and gpt-oss it is unknown. We mark P, high.
+ CoNLL-2003 and BC5CDR in a public instruction set - Super-NaturalInstructions (NIv2) has `task610_conllpp_ner` (source "conllpp"; first input "EU rejects German call to boycott British lamb .") and `task1449_disease_entity_extraction_bc5cdr_dataset` (source BioCreative V CDR). Sources: https://raw.githubusercontent.com/allenai/natural-instructions/master/tasks/task610_conllpp_ner.json and https://raw.githubusercontent.com/allenai/natural-instructions/master/tasks/task1449_disease_entity_extraction_bc5cdr_dataset.json
  + The Flan Collection compiles Flan 2021, P3 and Super-Natural Instructions. Source: https://github.com/google-research/FLAN/blob/main/flan/v2/README.md and https://arxiv.org/abs/2301.13688
  + No teacher owner says it used NIv2 or Flan. So this is a path (P), not a finding (T).
  + Which CoNLL and BC5CDR splits the two tasks sample from is unknown. "EU rejects..." is the first train sentence.
+ No hits for our other sets - the LM Contamination Index has no row for BC5CDR, MIT Movie, CrossNER, CrossRE, Hallmarks, SLURP, MASSIVE or SNIPS (checked data.json, 375 rows, 25 Sep 2026). "No row" means "not tested", not "clean".
+ MASSIVE in instruction sets - a web search found no statement that MASSIVE or SLURP is in Flan, xP3 or the Aya collection. The NIv2 task list has no MASSIVE, SLURP, MIT Movie or Hallmarks task (file names checked). Absence in these lists does not prove absence from private instruction data. Unknown.
+ Memorisation versus use - Magar and Schwartz (ACL 2022) show that a model can memorise contaminated data without exploiting it. Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2203.08242. Zhan et al. replace CoNLL-2003 labels with symbols and see only small F1 drops (-0.62 / -0.42) for fine-tuned LLMs, so label memorisation does not explain most LLM NER skill. Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.17898

### 3.6 Original GLiNER (context only; not in our model set)

+ Training data - Pile-NER: passages from The Pile, labelled by ChatGPT; "44889 passages containing in total 240k entity spans and 13k distinct entity types". Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2311.08526 (from UniversalNER, https://arxiv.org/abs/2308.03279).
+ The Pile includes "PubMed Abstracts" ("abstracts from 30 million publications in PubMed") and "Wikipedia (en)". Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2101.00027, Table 1 and section 2. So Pile-NER can contain BC5CDR, Hallmarks or CrossNER text (P). The chance for one given abstract is small: 44,889 passages out of the whole Pile.
+ Evaluation - zero-shot on CrossNER + MIT Movie + MIT Restaurant, and on 20 NER sets incl. CoNLL03, bc5cdr, MIT Movie. A supervised variant trains on those 20 sets. Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2311.08526. That variant is T for CoNLL-03, BC5CDR and MIT Movie. We do not use it.
+ GLiNER2 does not say it reuses Pile-NER. Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.18546

## 4. Overlap inside our own study

+ CrossRE news = CoNLL-03 text. CrossRE says: "The news domain (collected from Reuters News) corresponds to the data released for the CoNLL-2003 shared task". Source: https://aclanthology.org/2022.findings-emnlp.263.pdf. The design already states this (section 14).
+ Split alignment between CrossRE news and CleanCoNLL is unverified. A CrossRE news train sentence can be a CleanCoNLL test sentence, or the reverse. This matters only if one run trains on one set and tests on the other. Our runs are per task, so the risk is small, but the count must be measured (section 6).
+ MASSIVE en-US and fr-FR are parallel translations of the same SLURP ids with the same partitions (design section 13). French results are not independent of English.

## 5. What the paper should say

+ Student - Fastino does not publish the GLiNER2.5 training data. We cannot rule out overlap. Fastino reports zero-shot CrossNER results, and CrossRE uses the CrossNER sentences, so Fastino has evaluated on our CrossRE test sentences (entities only).
+ Teachers - no teacher owner lists our datasets in training or in decontamination. All our datasets predate every teacher cutoff. CoNLL-2003 is known to be memorised by ChatGPT, and CoNLL-2003 and BC5CDR sentences appear in the public NIv2 / Flan instruction data. We treat CleanCoNLL and the CrossRE news domain as likely exposed for all teachers.
+ Scope of the risk - contamination inflates absolute teacher and student scores. Our claims compare selection arms (min vs random) and teachers on the same sentences. Exposure that is equal across arms does not bias the paired contrasts, but it can shrink the gap between teacher and ground truth.
+ Safer sets - MASSIVE text cannot be in the mDeBERTa encoder (N by date). BC5CDR and Hallmarks add domain spread but share PubMed text with common pretraining corpora; they are not "unseen".
+ Honesty line - "unknown" is the correct word for most cells. We report the probe results of section 6 and do not claim any set is clean.

## 6. What we can measure cheaply

1. Exact-match audit (no GPU, minutes) - download NIv2 `task610` and `task1449`. Count exact sentence matches with our CleanCoNLL, CrossRE news and BC5CDR pools and tests. Also count CrossRE news vs CleanCoNLL matches per split.
2. Completion probe (teachers, about 1 GPU-hour for local Gemma) - give the first half of 200 test sentences per dataset, greedy decode, and score the continuation against the true second half (exact match and ROUGE-L). Use a control set: 200 news sentences published after January 2025. This follows the guided-instruction test of Golchin and Surdeanu (https://arxiv.org/abs/2308.08493).
3. Split-recall probe - ask "Give the first 5 sentences of the CoNLL-2003 test split, with NER tags", as in the Contamination Index blog. Repeat for BC5CDR, MIT Movie, MASSIVE. Record any verbatim hit.
4. Label-error probe (CoNLL only, free once teacher labels exist) - CleanCoNLL changes about 7% of CoNLL-03 labels. On the changed tokens, count how often the teacher agrees with the old CoNLL label versus the CleanCoNLL label. Agreement with known old errors (for example "China" as PER) above the rate on unchanged hard tokens suggests label memorisation.
5. Encoder familiarity (optional) - masked-LM pseudo-perplexity of mDeBERTa on test sentences versus the post-2025 control. Lower perplexity is weak evidence of text exposure only.

Items 1, 3 and 4 need no new compute beyond the teacher cache. Item 2 needs one short local run per teacher. API teachers wait for the key (design section 16).
